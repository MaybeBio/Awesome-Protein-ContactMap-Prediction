* Protenix-Mini

Lightweight inference is critical for biomolecular structure prediction and other downstream tasks, enabling efficient real-world deployment and inference-time scaling for large-scale applications. In this work, we address the challenge of balancing model efficiency and prediction accuracy by making several key modifications, 1) Multi-step AF3 sampler is replaced by a few-step ODE sampler, significantly reducing computational overhead for the diffusion module part during inference; 2) In the open-source Protenix framework, a subset of pairformer or diffusion transformer blocks doesn't make contributions to the final structure prediction, presenting opportunities for architectural pruning and lightweight redesign; 3) A model incorporating an ESM module is trained to substitute the conventional MSA module, reducing MSA preprocessing time. Building on these key insights, we present Protenix-Mini, a compact and optimized model designed for efficient protein structure prediction. This streamlined version incorporates a more efficient architectural design with a two-step Ordinary Differential Equation (ODE) sampling strategy. By eliminating redundant Transformer components and refining the sampling process, Protenix-Mini significantly reduces model complexity with slight accuracy drop. Evaluations on benchmark datasets demonstrate that it achieves high-fidelity predictions, with only a negligible 1 to 5 percent decrease in performance on benchmark datasets compared to its full-scale counterpart. This makes Protenix-Mini an ideal choice for applications where computational resources are limited but accurate structure prediction remains crucial.

> 轻量化推理对于生物分子结构预测及其他下游任务至关重要，它能够实现模型在真实场景下的高效部署，同时支持大规模应用的推理时扩展。本文针对模型效率与预测精度难以兼顾的难题，开展了多项关键改进：1）采用少步常微分方程采样器替换多步AF3采样器，大幅降低推理阶段扩散模块的计算开销；2）在开源Protenix框架中，部分Pairformer模块与扩散Transformer模块对最终结构预测无明显贡献，为模型结构剪枝与轻量化重构提供了可行空间；3）训练集成ESM模块的模型以替代传统多序列比对（MSA）模块，缩短多序列比对预处理耗时。基于以上关键发现，本文提出Protenix‑Mini，一款面向高效蛋白质结构预测的小型优化模型。该精简版本采用效率更高的架构设计，并使用两步常微分方程（ODE）采样策略。通过移除冗余Transformer组件并优化采样流程，Protenix‑Mini在精度仅有小幅下降的前提下显著降低了模型复杂度。基准数据集上的实验结果表明，该模型可实现高保真度预测；相较于完整版本，其在基准数据集上的性能仅出现1%‑5%的微小衰减。因此，对于计算资源有限，但仍需要高精度结构预测的应用场景，Protenix‑Mini是十分理想的选择。

* Protenix

In this technical report, we present Protenix, a comprehensive reproduction of AlphaFold3 (AF3), aimed at advancing the field of biomolecular structure prediction. Protenix tackles the challenges of predicting complex interactions involving proteins, ligands, and nucleic acids, while enhancing accessibility and reproducibility. Across diverse benchmarks, including PoseBusters V2, low-homology PDB sets, and CASP15 RNA, Protenix achieves state-of-the-art performance in protein-ligand, protein-protein, and protein-nucleic acid predictions. We also address limitations, such as potential memorization effects, and outline future directions for improvement. By open-sourcing Protenix, we aim to democratize advanced structure prediction tools and accelerate interdisciplinary research in computational biology and drug discovery.

> 本技术报告介绍了Protenix，该模型对AlphaFold3（AF3）进行完整复现，旨在推动生物分子结构预测领域的发展。Protenix致力于解决蛋白质、配体与核酸之间复杂相互作用的预测难题，同时提升工具的易用性与可复现性。在PoseBusters V2、低同源性PDB数据集、CASP15 RNA等多项基准测试中，Protenix在蛋白质‑配体、蛋白质‑蛋白质以及蛋白质‑核酸预测任务上取得了业界顶尖水平的性能。本文同时探讨了模型存在的局限，例如潜在的记忆效应，并指明后续改进方向。通过开源Protenix，我们希望让先进的结构预测工具惠及更多研究者，推动计算生物学与药物发现领域的跨学科研究加速发展。


* Protenix-v1

We introduce Protenix-v1 (PX-v1), the first fully open-source structure prediction model to attain superior performance to AlphaFold3 while strictly adhering to the same training data cutoff, model size, and inference budget. Beyond standard evaluations, we highlight the effectiveness of inference-time scaling behavior of Protenix-v1, demonstrating that increasing the sampling budget yields consistent improvements in prediction quality—a behavior previously observed in AlphaFold3 and largely absent from prior open-source models. In addition to improved accuracy, Protenix-v1 incorporates key capabilities including protein template integration and RNA MSA support. Furthermore, to better support real-world applications such as drug discovery, we additionally release Protenix-v1-20250630, a variant trained on a larger dataset (cutoff: June 30, 2025), delivering further improved prediction accuracy. Finally, we identify limitations in existing benchmarking practices and provide updated evaluation tools and year-stratified benchmarks to support more reliable and transparent assessment. Collectively, these contributions provide a robust foundation for the Protenix series and the broader field.

> 本文提出Protenix‑v1（PX‑v1），这是首款在严格沿用相同训练数据截止条件、模型规模与推理开销的前提下，实现性能超越AlphaFold3的完全开源结构预测模型。除常规评估外，我们着重验证了Protenix‑v1的推理时刻缩放特性的效果，证明增大采样开销能够持续提升预测质量；该特性此前仅在AlphaFold3中被观测到，而在以往开源模型中基本不存在。除预测精度提升外，Protenix‑v1具备多项核心能力，包括蛋白质模板融合与RNA多序列比对（MSA）支持。此外，为更好服务药物发现等实际应用场景，我们发布了Protenix‑v1‑20250630版本，该变体基于规模更大的数据集训练（数据截止时间：2025年6月30日），进一步提升了预测精度。最后，我们指出现有基准测试方案存在的缺陷，并提供更新后的评估工具以及按年份分层的基准数据集，以实现更可靠、更透明的模型评测。综上，以上成果为Protenix系列模型以及整个相关研究领域奠定了坚实基础。




> 生物分子建模技术的进步拓展了结构预测与设计模型可解决的问题范围。本文展示了来自
Protenix‑v2的实验结果，该系统兼顾高精度结构预测与生物分子设计两大能力。在结构预测方面，Protenix‑v2的抗体‑抗原预测成功率较Protenix‑v1最高提升13个百分点，同时

* Protenix-v2

Advances in biomolecular modeling have broadened the range of problems addressable by structure prediction and design models. Here, we present results from Protenix-v2, a system spanning high-accuracy structure prediction and biomolecular design. On the structure prediction side, Protenix-v2 achieves antibody-antigen success rates with up to 13-point gains over Protenix-v1, while 5-seed performance surpasses previous 1000-seed results. On the design side, Protenix-v2 demonstrates a 100% target-level success rate in novelty-controlled VHH-Fc campaigns, reaching hit rates up to 48%. Crucially, the model enables hit discovery on difficult GPCR targets with hit rates of 16%–88% (VHH-Fc) and up to 50% (mAb) under 16–30 testing budgets per target. Resulting hits show high developability and diversity. Beyond antibody tasks, we report improved ligand-related plausibility and successful cross-variant SARS-CoV-2 spike RBD mini-binder design. These results establish Protenix-v2 as a robust and powerful model for accelerated drug discovery.

生物分子建模技术的进步拓展了结构预测与设计模型可解决的问题范围。本文展示了来自Protenix‑v2系统的实验结果，该系统兼具高精度结构预测与生物分子设计能力。在结构预测方面，Protenix‑v2的抗体‑抗原预测成功率相比Protenix‑v1最高提升13个百分点，同时5次随机种子的性能超越了以往1000次随机种子的结果。在分子设计方面，在受控新颖性的VHH‑Fc设计项目中，Protenix‑v2实现了100%的靶点层面成功率，有效分子命中率最高可达48%。至关重要的是，该模型能够针对难度较高的GPCR靶点完成有效分子挖掘；在每个靶点仅开展16‑30次实验测试的条件下，VHH‑Fc的命中率为16%‑88%，单克隆抗体命中率最高达50%。筛选得到的候选分子具备优异的可开发性与多样性。除抗体相关任务外，本研究还实现了配体相关合理性的提升，并完成可结合不同变异株的新冠病毒刺突蛋白RBD小型结合分子的设计。上述结果证明Protenix‑v2是一套稳定、性能强大的模型，可助力药物研发进程加速。
