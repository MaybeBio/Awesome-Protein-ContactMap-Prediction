# AlphaFold-Multimer

## Abstract

While the vast majority of well-structured single protein chains can now be predicted to high accuracy due to the recent AlphaFold [1] model, the prediction of multi-chain protein complexes remains a challenge in many cases. In this work, we demonstrate that an AlphaFold model trained specifically for multimeric inputs of known stoichiometry, which we call AlphaFold-Multimer, significantly increases accuracy of predicted multimeric interfaces over input-adapted single-chain AlphaFold while maintaining high intra-chain accuracy. On a benchmark dataset of 17 heterodimer proteins without templates (introduced in [2]) we achieve at least medium accuracy (DockQ [3] ≥ 0.49) on 13 targets and high accuracy (DockQ ≥ 0.8) on 7 targets, compared to 9 targets of at least medium accuracy and 4 of high accuracy for the previous state of the art system (an AlphaFold-based system from [2]). We also predict structures for a large dataset of 4,446 recent protein complexes, from which we score all non-redundant interfaces with low template identity. For heteromeric interfaces we successfully predict the interface (DockQ ≥ 0.23) in 70% of cases, and produce high accuracy predictions (DockQ ≥ 0.8) in 26% of cases, an improvement of +27 and +14 percentage points over the flexible linker modification of AlphaFold [4] respectively. For homomeric inter-faces we successfully predict the interface in 72% of cases, and produce high accuracy predictions in 36% of cases, an improvement of +8 and +7 percentage points respectively.

> 尽管依托新近推出的AlphaFold[1]模型，目前绝大多数结构良好的单蛋白链都能实现高精度预测，但在许多场景下，多链蛋白复合物的预测仍存在不小挑战。本文研究表明：我们构建了一款专门针对已知化学计量比的多聚体输入进行训练的AlphaFold模型，命名为AlphaFold-Multimer；相较于经过输入适配改造的单链AlphaFold，该模型可显著提升多聚体蛋白界面的预测精度，同时保留很高的链内预测精度。
在一个包含17个无模板异二聚体蛋白的基准数据集（文献[2]提出）上，本方法有13个目标达到至少中等精度（DockQ[3] ≥ 0.49）、7个目标达到高精度（DockQ ≥ 0.8）；而此前最优方法（文献[2]提出的基于AlphaFold的系统）仅9个目标达到至少中等精度、4个目标达到高精度。我们还对包含4446个新近解析蛋白复合物的大规模数据集开展结构预测，并对其中所有模板同源性较低的非冗余蛋白界面进行打分。针对异源多聚体界面，本方法可在70%的案例中实现界面的有效预测（DockQ ≥ 0.23），26%的案例实现高精度预测（DockQ ≥ 0.8），相比采用柔性连接子改造的AlphaFold[4]，两项指标分别提升27个百分点、14个百分点。针对同源多聚体界面，本方法可在72%的案例中实现界面的有效预测，36%的案例实现高精度预测，两项指标分别提升8个百分点、7个百分点。

## Model/Method

- **Input:** The multimer pipeline accepts a FASTA with N chains (not limited to two), processed per-chain via `_process_single_chain` in `pipeline_multimer.DataPipeline` . For heteromers (2+ unique sequences), it additionally builds a cross-chain **paired MSA** from UniProt hits via `_all_seq_msa_features`, with the pairing/matching logic implemented in `create_paired_features` / `pair_sequences` in `msa_pairing.py` , exploiting co-evolution signal across chains. IDRs lacking deep MSA coverage tend to receive lower pLDDT as a natural consequence of the model's confidence estimation, though this isn't an explicitly coded rule.
- **Output:** The model's `prediction_result` (from `RunModel.predict`) is 3D atom coordinates in `structure_module.final_atom_positions`, saved to PDB via `protein.from_prediction` in `run_alphafold.py` . `distogram.logits`/`bin_edges` are only an auxiliary intermediate head, not the terminal output . Confidence metrics ship pre-calibrated, no manual re-calibration needed: per-residue `plddt`, pairwise `predicted_aligned_error` (which naturally reveals inter-/intra-chain blocks), global `ptm`, and — multimer-specific — `iptm` for interface confidence, combined into `ranking_confidence` for model selection.
